Lelap Privacy Policy

Effective 25 November 2026 · App: Lelap (com.refjz.lelap) · Bahasa Indonesia

Lelap puts rarely used Android apps to sleep (disables them until you need them), wakes them up on demand, and includes an optional home screen launcher and device tools. This policy explains what data Lelap uses, what leaves your device, and the choices you have.

Summary

  • Lelap has no account and does not sell your personal data.
  • App management, launcher, notification and usage features run entirely on your device.
  • Lelap shows ads from Google AdMob in the manager app (not on the launcher home screen). AdMob collects some device data, described below.
  • If you turn on the weather widget, your approximate location is sent to Open-Meteo to get the current weather.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of Lelap is supported by ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID and app set ID.
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address.
  • Ad interactions (impressions and taps), app diagnostics and performance data.

In the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland and other regions where it is required, Lelap asks for your consent with Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Lelap → Settings → Ad privacy. In US states with privacy laws, you can opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information through the same form when it is offered. You can also reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device settings.

Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Data processed only on your device

  • Installed apps (name, icon, enabled state): to list, put to sleep, wake, and launch apps.
  • App usage statistics (only if you grant Usage access): to sort apps by use and suggest apps to put to sleep.
  • Notifications (only if you enable notification access for badges, notification history or digest): Lelap counts notifications for icon badges and, if you turn on notification history, keeps the title and text of up to 300 recent notifications for at most 3 days so you can read dismissed ones. This stays in Lelap's private storage and is never sent anywhere.
  • Calendar (optional agenda widget): the next event's title and time are shown on the home screen, not stored or sent.
  • Contacts (optional launcher search): contact names are searched on the device while you type; not copied, stored or sent.
  • Wireless debugging pairing key: created and stored on the device so Lelap can connect to ADB on that same device.
  • Settings and backups: folders, schedules, launcher layout and themes; backup files are only written to a location you choose.
  • Crash report: the last crash is stored locally and can only be copied by you from Settings.

All of this is deleted when you uninstall Lelap.

Weather widget (optional)

If you turn on the weather widget and grant approximate location, Lelap sends your location rounded to about 1 km to Open-Meteo at most every 30 minutes to fetch the current weather. No device identifier or account is included.

Special permissions and services

  • See all installed apps: core function of an app manager and launcher.
  • Wireless debugging / ADB: Lelap pairs with your own device's ADB to disable and enable apps without root. The connection is local to your device.
  • Local VPN (only if you use "Block internet" per app): Lelap creates a local VPN that drops traffic from the apps you block. It does not connect to any server and does not inspect, log or send your traffic.
  • Accessibility service (only in the APK distributed outside Google Play, and only if you turn it on): reads the Settings app's Wireless debugging pairing dialog to fill in the 6-digit code automatically. It reads no other app, types nothing and sends nothing.
  • Display over other apps: for the optional edge side panel.
  • Biometrics: to unlock apps you lock in Lelap.
  • Delete apps / install APKs: only when you ask, with the system confirmation.

Security

Ad traffic to Google and weather requests to Open-Meteo use encrypted HTTPS. Lelap's internal shell server listens only on the device's loopback address and requires a random secret token.

Children

Lelap is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Your choices

  • Change ad consent in Settings → Ad privacy, or reset your Advertising ID in device settings.
  • Revoke any optional permission at any time in Android settings.
  • Uninstall Lelap to delete all data it stores on your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
